02 / UNDERSTAND THE PROOF

Why the construction works.

01

Choose one literal per variable

The first three target digits are 1. Each truth assignment includes exactly one corresponding variable number.

02

Use slack to reach 4

A satisfied clause has 1, 2, or 3 true literals. Slack numbers worth 1 and 2 can fill the remaining amount.

03

Detect an unsatisfied clause

A false clause starts at 0. Its two slack values sum to at most 3, so that clause digit cannot reach the target 4.

The formula is satisfiable if and only if some subset sums to 1114444. Every digit can be checked independently because the construction prevents decimal carries.
