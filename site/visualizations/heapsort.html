Build and sort a max heap

Proof and complexity

With 1-based lecture indexing, parent(i)=⌊i/2⌋, left(i)=2i, right(i)=2i+1. A max heap obeys A[parent(i)] ≥ A[i]. MAX-HEAPIFY pushes a possibly undersized key downward in O(height) time. BUILD-MAX-HEAP is O(n) because most nodes have small height; HEAPSORT is Θ(n log n).

After each extraction, the suffix is sorted and all its values are at least the remaining heap elements. Try duplicate keys, a descending input, and a one-element input.
