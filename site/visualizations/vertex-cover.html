Why this guarantees a 2-approximation

The chosen edges form a maximal matching: no two selected edges share a vertex, and every original edge touches an endpoint selected by the algorithm. A valid vertex cover needs at least one endpoint of every matching edge. Thus OPT ≥ |M|, while the algorithm selects exactly 2|M| vertices. Therefore |C| = 2|M| ≤ 2 OPT.

A maximal matching need not be a maximum matching, and a 2-approximation guarantee does not mean the returned cover always costs twice the optimum. The exact optimum displayed here is computed by exhaustive search for these small teaching graphs; it is not part of the polynomial-time approximation algorithm.
