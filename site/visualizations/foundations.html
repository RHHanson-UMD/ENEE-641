The Master Theorem and inductive substitution require matching hypotheses. An O-bound alone need not be tight; check a corresponding lower bound.

Merge sort — Chapter 2

Correctness comes from recursively sorting each half and maintaining sorted output during MERGE. The recurrence T(n)=2T(n/2)+Θ(n) gives Θ(n log n).

Lomuto partition — Chapter 7

After processing each j, the prefix of examined values at or before boundary i is ≤ pivot, and examined values to its right are > pivot. The final swap puts the pivot at its sorted partition position. Quicksort has quadratic worst-case behavior even though randomization improves expected work.

Selection — Chapter 9

Deterministic SELECT groups elements into fives, recursively chooses a median of medians, and partitions around it. The chosen pivot discards a constant fraction, yielding worst-case O(n). Randomized SELECT instead has expected O(n) but quadratic worst case.
