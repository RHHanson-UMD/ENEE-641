Invariant and deletion cases

Inorder traversal prints all keys in nondecreasing order. Search follows left or right links until a match or NIL; cost is proportional to tree height. Removing a node with two children replaces its key with the minimum key in its right subtree, then deletes that successor there. A leaf or one-child deletion needs no successor replacement.

The lecture demonstrates that two differently shaped trees with keys 2,3,5,5,6,7,8 can have heights 2 and 4 yet share identical inorder output. This page uses independent starting keys; it does not claim its tree drawing matches the source figure.
